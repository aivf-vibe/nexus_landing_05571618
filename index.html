Frequently Asked Questions

How does StartFlow's unified API work?

StartFlow provides a single API endpoint that routes your requests to multiple LLM providers. Our intelligent routing system automatically handles fallbacks, load balancing, and provider selection based on uptime, latency, and your preferences.

What payment methods do you support?

We support UPI payments for Indian users, making it easy to add credits to your account. No subscriptions required - you only pay for the tokens you use with transparent pricing in rupees.

How do you ensure high uptime and reliability?

We continuously monitor all providers with real-time uptime tracking. Our automatic failover system routes requests to healthy providers, and we provide transparent uptime statistics for each provider.

Can I become a provider on StartFlow?

Yes! StartFlow welcomes new providers. You can submit your hosted models through our Provider Dashboard. We test all submissions for 3 hours at random intervals before approval.

What data policies do you follow?

We provide transparent data policy controls. Each provider specifies their logging and training data usage policies, which are clearly displayed. You can choose providers based on your data privacy requirements.
